Programme Overview

Train for Five Months. Then Choose Your Track.

The OPC Launchpad is a single, fully integrated Kydon programme, delivered in collaboration with Singapore Polytechnic. Training runs full-time across 8 modules and 580.5 hours, combining classroom learning with asynchronous e-learning. On graduation, you earn 8 Certificates of Completion and choose the pathway that fits your ambition.

Founder

Launch Your Own OPC

Build and run your own AI-powered One Person Company — serving real clients, operating lean, and growing on your own terms. Kydon's mentorship and ecosystem remain available to support your journey.

AI Specialist

Become a Professional AI Operator

Step into a fast-growing market as a qualified AI specialist — helping businesses build, deploy, and run AI-powered systems. The skills you build here are exactly what organisations are looking for right now.

Course Structure

8 Modules · 580.5 Hours

01 Gear Up: Essential Career Skills 43.5h

The foundation of the OPC mindset — develop the thinking patterns of an AI-leveraged operator and the on-ramp to everything that follows.

  • Adopt the operator mindset and spot high-value opportunities to pursue
  • Identify and automate the repetitive, low-value work in your day
  • Use GenAI tools to boost everyday business productivity
  • Build fluency across the core AI tools used throughout the programme
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Gear up! Essential Skills for Career Transformation and Professional Growth
02 Programming for Data Science 64h

Python programming from the ground up with a focus on data-science applications and the core professional libraries.

  • Write Python from first principles for data-science tasks
  • Manipulate and analyse datasets using NumPy and Pandas
  • Create clear charts and plots with Matplotlib
  • Work confidently with both structured and unstructured data
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Introduction to Programming for Data Science
03 Database Management Systems 72h

A working understanding of how data is stored, organised, and retrieved at scale.

  • Design and query relational and non-relational databases
  • Explain data-warehousing fundamentals and when to apply them
  • Build ETL processes to move and transform data reliably
  • Integrate data from multiple sources into a single store
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Fundamentals of Database Management Systems
04 Data Wrangling and Visualisation 76h

Take raw, messy data and transform it into clean, analysis-ready datasets and clear visualisations.

  • Clean and prepare messy, raw data into analysis-ready datasets with Python
  • Diagnose and resolve common data-quality issues
  • Summarise and interpret data using descriptive statistics
  • Communicate insights through effective visualisations with Seaborn
🎓 Certificate of Completion in From Chaos to Clarity: Data Wrangling for Analysis and Visualisation
05 Building Business Solutions with Low-Code 76h

Build the core tool of your own OPC using no-code and AI-assisted development, from setup to deployment.

  • Build a working business tool using no-code and low-code platforms
  • Assemble both the frontend and backend of your OPC's core tool
  • Accelerate development with AI-assisted building techniques
  • Deploy your solution with scalability and maintainability in mind
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Building Business Solutions with Low-Code Platforms
06 Unlocking AI 96h

The LLM engine room — reason with data and make decisions by prompting AI, culminating in a working AI brain for your OPC.

  • Understand how large language models work under the hood
  • Engineer effective prompts to get reliable, repeatable results
  • Design AI agents that carry out multi-step tasks
  • Build a RAG knowledge base grounded in your own data
  • Apply AI ethically and responsibly in a business context
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Unlocking AI: From Data to Predictions and Beyond
07 Integrating Data and AI with Low-Code 96h

Assemble the full integrated AI business engine into a single functioning system.

  • Automate end-to-end workflows across your business systems
  • Integrate third-party services through their APIs
  • Add a GenAI layer that powers intelligent automation
  • Build an operating dashboard to monitor your OPC
  • Pass the Without-You Test: prove your business runs without you for 48 hours
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Integrating Data and AI with Low-Code Development Platform
08 Leap Forward: Skills for Growth 57h

Graduate ready to step into your chosen pathway, with the narrative and readiness for the D-Day Showcase.

  • Prepare your OPC for launch and sustainable growth
  • Position your brand and craft a go-to-market strategy
  • Make data-driven decisions with AI support
  • Pitch with confidence and present at the D-Day Showcase
🎓 Certificate of Completion in Leap Forward! Essential Skills for Career Transformation and Professional Growth

Total: 580.5 hours · 8 Certificates of Completion · Full-time over 5 months.
