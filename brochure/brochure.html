Programme Overview

Train for Five Months. Then Choose Your Track.

The OPC Launchpad is a single, fully integrated Kydon programme, delivered in collaboration with Singapore Polytechnic. Training runs full-time across 8 modules and 580.5 hours, combining classroom learning with asynchronous e-learning. On graduation, you earn 8 Certificates of Completion and choose the pathway that fits your ambition.

Founder

Launch Your Own OPC

Build and run your own AI-powered One Person Company — serving real clients, operating lean, and growing on your own terms. Kydon's mentorship and ecosystem remain available to support your journey.

AI Specialist

Become a Professional AI Operator

Step into a fast-growing market as a qualified AI specialist — helping businesses build, deploy, and run AI-powered systems. The skills you build here are exactly what organisations are looking for right now.
